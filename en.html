Full Professor, School of Artificial Intelligence, Shenzhen Technology University · Lead of OpenFEEL

Xiaojiang Peng (IEEE Senior Member) is a full professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shenzhen Technology University, and head of the Department of Intelligent Science and Technology (founder of the university's AI undergraduate program). He is a master's supervisor at Shenzhen University, a Shenzhen "Peacock" talent, and has been selected for the Stanford University top 2% most-cited scientists list every year since 2022.

He received his Ph.D. from Southwest Jiaotong University. He was a postdoctoral researcher at Inria (LEAR/THOTH, France, with Cordelia Schmid) and Idiap Research Institute, Switzerland (with Francois Fleuret) from 2015 to 2017, and an associate professor at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, from 2018 to 2020. His research interests include affective computing, computer vision, multimodal large language models, embodied intelligence, and generative AI. He serves as an Associate Editor of IEEE Transactions on Affective Computing and Empathic Computing, and has published 100+ papers in top venues such as IEEE TIP, CVPR, ICCV and NeurIPS, with 8,000+ Google Scholar citations and an H-index of 35. He has led more than 10 projects including NSFC General Program, holds 10 granted patents, and has received the Guangdong Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award (First Prize), the Wu Wenjun AI Science and Technology Progress Award (Second Prize) and other honors.

Research

Building an end-to-end research pipeline — from multimodal perception and emotion understanding to embodied interaction and generation — at the frontier of affective and embodied learning (OpenFEEL).

Affective Computing

Facial expression recognition, multimodal emotion understanding, emotion LLMs and speech emotion.

Computer Vision

Face recognition, action recognition, 3D reconstruction and human-object interaction.

Multimodal LLMs

Multimodal emotion understanding LLMs, instruction tuning and alignment.

Embodied Intelligence

Robotic manipulation, embodied agents and interaction with the physical world.

Generative AI

Diffusion models, controllable image editing and content forgery detection.
